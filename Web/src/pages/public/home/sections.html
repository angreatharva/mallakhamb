The rope form grew out of practice on cane. When good cane became scarce, cotton rope replaced it.

Milestones

  1. 1135CE

    First described in the Sanskrit text Manasollasa, by the Western Chalukya king Someshvara III.

  2. Early1800s

    Revived in Pune by Balambhatt Dada Deodhar, teacher to Peshwa Bajirao II.

  3. 1958

    First competitive appearance, at the National Gymnastics Championships.

  4. 1962

    First national championship, in Gwalior.

  5. 1981

    The Mallakhamb Federation of India holds the first all-India nationals and sets the rules.

  6. 2013

    Madhya Pradesh makes Mallakhamb its state sport.

  7. 2019

    The first World Mallakhamb Championship, in Mumbai.

  8. 2021

    Pole Mallakhamb opens to girls at the Nationals.

The three apparatus

Mallakhamb is performed on three apparatus: a vertical pole fixed in the ground, a shorter pole hung from hooks and a chain, and a cotton rope. Each has its own measurements, and its own way of fighting back.

Pole

A vertical pole of teak or sheesham, fixed in the ground and smeared with castor oil to control friction. It has a head, a neck and a body that tapers toward the top.
